Nuŋtsɔ Nyɔŋmɔ ji kpojiemɔ kɛ hewale kɛha wɔ fɛɛ.
Wɔkɛ kpokpoi kɛ palm nut soup yeɔ afahyɛ lɛ be mli.
Wɔsumɔɔ ni wɔkase Twi, Ewe, Ga kɛ Fante wiemɔi lɛ.
Hɔmɔwɔ afahyɛ lɛ ji afahyɛ kɛse kɛha Ga mashi bii fɛɛ.
Nii Mei kɛ Naa Mei ji gbɛtsɔɔlɔi kpakpai kɛha bii.
Nitsulɔ lɛ tsu nii waa kɛha e-shia kɛ e-weku.
Nyɔŋmɔ ajɔɔ Ghana maŋ lɛ kɛ emli bii fɛɛ daa gbi.
Kɛ otee Makola jra nɔ lɛ obaana nibii vovoo pii.
Gbalamɔ kɛ suɔmɔ yɛ weku lɛ mli be fɛɛ be.
Gbɛkɛbii lɛ mii shwe bɔɔl yɛ agona lɛ nɔ gbɛkɛ nɛɛ.
Ga mashi ji he kpo kɛha kusum kɛ blema nibii pii.
Maŋtsɛ lɛ wie hewale wiemɔ kɛtsɔɔ maŋbii lɛ fɛɛ.
Ganyobi ji mi ni mihe yɛ fɛo yɛ he fɛɛ he kɔŋkɔŋ.
Mɛi pii basara Ga maŋ lɛ be ni ayeɔ afahyɛ lɛ.
Ga wiemɔ lɛ yɛ fɛo waa ni eŋɔɔ mɛi pii naabu.
Tsu nii kpakpa koni onya hewalɛ kɛ nibii kpakpai.

Kɔnu sia wɔwɔ lɔ mɔ siwo dzi wotona le azã la ɖuɖu me la tata ɖe eme tso Akropong ƒe titina si nye Okuapeman ƒe toxɔdu yi ɖe fiasã si woyɔna be Amanprobi la me.
Wowɔ esia kple susu be woatsɔ ado nusrɔ̃lawo ƒe ta me deto bubu ku ɖe nyatinyagbe, alɔdonyagbewo kple nyati vevi siwo le nuŋlɔɖiwo me la ŋu kpɔ.
Eɖe susu tso wɔƒe vovovo siwo dezãwo ɖuɖu wɔna le nuto eve me la ŋu gblɔ to gɔmesese nyuitɔ ku ɖe nuto vovovoawo ƒe dezãwo ɖuɖu nu ɖeɖe fia me.
